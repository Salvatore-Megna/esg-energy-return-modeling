\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Research question}\par
Do lagged ESG scores help predict one-year benchmark-relative returns, and how do energy company groups compare on risk-adjusted returns?
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Data and methods}\par
Twelve listed US and European energy companies; monthly observations spanning 2013--2024 across three political periods. Industry groups are utilities/transition and oil and gas.

Random Forest regressions with temporal evaluation, sector-relative returns, equal-weight portfolios and paired block-bootstrap confidence intervals.
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Selected results}\par
\begin{itemize}
\item In the updated evaluation, out-of-sample $R^2$ is negative in five of six region-period models.
\item The single positive test is US 2017--2020 ($R^2=0.396$), based on only eight test months.
\item EU 2017--2020 information-ratio difference: 1.676 (95\% bootstrap interval 0.196--3.247).
\end{itemize}
\par
\par
\vspace{1.15em}
\noindent{\bfseries Interpretation and limitations}\par
Utility versus oil-and-gas membership confounds ESG with industry. Return construction, short test windows and multiple comparisons limit inference. The separate original 2025 report retains its historical figures.
